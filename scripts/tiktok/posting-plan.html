Posting one

  1. Get the videos onto your phone: AirDrop this batch's folder from your Mac to your iPhone (right-click the folder → Share → AirDrop). They land in Files or Photos.
  2. In TikTok, tap + and pick that day's video. The videos are silent on purpose: add a trending sound in TikTok's Sounds. Sound matters a lot for reach.
  3. Copy the text and hashtags below and paste them in, then post. Three to six hashtags is plenty; the general football ones bring reach, the mode ones bring the right people. Early evening (6 to 9pm UK) tends to do best for football.
  4. Post the same video to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Instagram lets you link the app in your bio straight away.
  5. Reply to comments, especially guesses and scores. Replies push a video to more people.
  • The end card says "Search Footballdle" on the App Store, because TikTok only allows a bio link after about 1,000 followers. Footballdle is the top result for that search.
  • Every video uses a puzzle that's already finished, never today's. Tick each one off as you post it (saved in this browser only).
